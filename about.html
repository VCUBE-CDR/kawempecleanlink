Who we are

About Kawempe CleanLink

A student project exploring how a simple website can make waste-management information easier to access.

The problem we are addressing

Waste management depends on responsible household practices, reliable collection and communication between residents and service providers. When waste is not stored or collected appropriately, it can create unpleasant and unhealthy surroundings.

Kawempe CleanLink focuses on helping residents find practical guidance and organise information about visible concerns. It does not collect rubbish, dispatch trucks, impose penalties or replace KCCA and licensed collectors.

Who may use the website?

  • Residents and tenants
  • Shop owners and small businesses
  • Community leaders
  • Authorised waste collectors and community groups
Our purpose
To support awareness and communication, not to promise immediate collection or official action.
