Practical guidance

Learn about waste

Simple habits can help reduce litter, improve storage and make waste easier to manage.

🍃

Organic waste

Examples include fruit and vegetable peels, food leftovers and garden waste. Keep it separate where a suitable collection or composting option exists.

♻️

Recyclable materials

Clean and dry paper, cardboard, metal cans, glass and some plastics may be recyclable where local facilities accept them.

🗑️

Residual waste

Some mixed or contaminated materials may not be suitable for recycling. Keep them contained and follow local collection guidance.

Store waste safely

  • Use a strong container with a lid.
  • Keep waste away from children and animals.
  • Avoid leaving waste in drainage channels or on roadsides.
  • Do not burn mixed household waste.
  • Follow the collection schedule provided by your authorised service provider.

Reduce, reuse and recycle

  • Reduce: avoid unnecessary packaging and single-use items.
  • Reuse: use durable bags and containers more than once.
  • Recycle: separate clean materials accepted by local recyclers.
  • Compost: consider suitable organic waste only where safe and practical.
